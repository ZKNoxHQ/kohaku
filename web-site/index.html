Railgun in the browser

Wallet
Sync, balance, private transfers signed on a Ledger (USB or Bluetooth), sent through ERC-4337 or a broadcaster over Waku. Viewer
History and Proof of Innocence status of an account from its viewing key, and the state of the Railgun network.

Static pages: everything runs in this browser (Rust compiled to WebAssembly). Keys never leave the page; only the RPC, the POI node, the bundler and the Waku network are contacted.
